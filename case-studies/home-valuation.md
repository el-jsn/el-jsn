# Home valuation for Realtris

I'm developing a residential valuation system for Realtris, integrated into its live home valuation experience. Users start with an address and confirm property information to request an automated estimate.

[Try the home valuation tool](https://realtris.ca/whats-my-home-worth)

## My contribution

My work focuses on the valuation experience, the quality of its results, and how limitations are communicated to users. The project is live, and I continue improving it.

## The challenges

Property information can be incomplete or inconsistent, and some homes are harder to assess than others. A useful valuation experience needs to account for those differences without giving users a misleading sense of certainty.

The work involves handling imperfect information, evaluating result quality, and making it clear when a property needs professional review.

## The user experience

The tool provides an estimated value, a value range, and supporting context. Some cases may require professional review rather than an automated estimate.

The estimate gives users a starting point for understanding their property's value. It is not a formal appraisal or a guaranteed selling price.
